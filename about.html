About us

About Farmers & Trustees Trust

We bring agricultural support, family legacy planning, employment and enterprise development together, so that communities can find help in one place.

Our mission

To empower communities through agriculture, family legacy planning, employment creation, entrepreneurship, and sustainable development.

Our vision

One integrated ecosystem connecting farmers, families, students, businesses, startups, investors, employers and development partners.

Our core values

Integrity

We are honest with members and partners, and we handle family and financial information with care.

Accountability

We keep proper records and report openly on what we do and how resources are used.

Innovation

We use practical tools, including digital ones, where they make life easier for our members.

Community Service

We put the needs of farmers, families and young people first.

Sustainability

We support farming and business practices that last for the next generation.

Who we serve

Farmers

Market access, advisory services and succession planning.

Families

Asset documentation, beneficiary registration and legacy planning.

Students and graduates

Internships, attachments, jobs and skills training.

Businesses and investors

Startup support, recruitment and investment connections.
